# Olivia's Egg Garden

A personal reward app where a kindergartener turns the stars she earns at school into hatching eggs and a growing collection of characters.

## Stars

**Star**:
The unit of reward a child earns at school for how she performed that day; between 0 and 3 per School Day.
_Avoid_: Point, coin, credit

**School Day**:
Any Monday to Friday; the only days a Star Entry can be made. Holidays are simply days with no Star Entry.

**Star Entry**:
The single record of Stars (0 to 3) earned on one School Day, tapped in by the child and approved by a parent. There is at most one per School Day.
_Avoid_: Deposit, submission, log

**Parent Gate**:
A check only an adult can pass, standing between the child and anything that changes Stars or settings.
_Avoid_: Lock, PIN, admin

**Parent Area**:
The adult-only part of the app, behind the Parent Gate, where Star Entries are added or corrected and settings and backups are managed.
_Avoid_: Settings, admin panel, dashboard

## Eggs and characters

**Egg**:
A container that fills with Stars until it reaches its Hatch Target. Only one Egg is active at a time, and an Egg can never be lost or harmed. Stars beyond the Hatch Target carry over into the next Egg.
_Avoid_: Mystery box, pack

**Egg Family**:
A group of Characters that share an Egg pattern; the child picks her next Egg by family, and the exact Character inside is a surprise.
_Avoid_: Type, category, set

**Hatch Target**:
The number of Stars an Egg needs before it hatches.
_Avoid_: Threshold, cost, price

**Hatch**:
The moment an Egg reaches its Hatch Target and reveals a Character.
_Avoid_: Unlock, open, reveal

**Character**:
A named collectible creature that comes out of an Egg. Once hatched, a Character is never taken back.
_Avoid_: Pet, monster, critter, collectible

**Collection**:
The full, fixed set of Characters, both hatched and not yet hatched.
_Avoid_: Inventory, album, pokédex

**Garden**:
The place where hatched Characters live and the active Egg rests in its nest.
_Avoid_: Home, world, habitat
