# 07: Fluffy family art

**What to build:** Polished, original SVG art for the 5 Characters in the Fluffy Egg Family (baby animals): Bun-Bun the bunny, Pip the chick, Mochi the kitten, Hazel the hedgehog, Pudding the puppy. They replace the placeholders, so they appear in the Garden, the hatch celebration and the Collection. The brief is to *wow a 5-year-old*: they should be cute, rounded, expressive and charming, clearly richer than simple shapes, and harmonise with the cream Egg with pastel spots.

**Blocked by:** 03 (Egg Families and choosing the next Egg)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Each of the 5 Characters has an original SVG with a distinct silhouette, a colour palette and a friendly face. None copies an existing franchise character.
- [ ] Each has a gentle idle animation (bobbing or breathing) and blinks at random intervals.
- [ ] Each has a unique tap dance of about 1–2 seconds that suits who it is (for example, Bun-Bun hops).
- [ ] Each also has a silhouette version for the Collection's not-yet-hatched state.
- [ ] Each Character uses the catalog's art interface, so no screen needs to change when its art is added.
- [ ] They look crisp from small Garden size up to full-screen reveal size, on phone and tablet.
- [ ] The animations stay smooth on a mid-range phone with all 15 Characters in the Garden.
