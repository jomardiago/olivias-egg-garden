# 08: Sparkly family art

**What to build:** Polished, original SVG art for the 5 Characters in the Sparkly Egg Family (magical creatures): Twinkle the unicorn, Ember the baby dragon, Glimmer the fairy fox, Puff the cloud sprite, Pippa the pegasus. They replace the placeholders, so they appear in the Garden, the hatch celebration and the Collection. The brief is to *wow a 5-year-old*: they should be cute, rounded, expressive and charming, clearly richer than simple shapes, and harmonise with the lavender Egg with glitter stars.

**Blocked by:** 03 (Egg Families and choosing the next Egg)

**Status:** resolved

- [ ] Each of the 5 Characters has an original SVG with a distinct silhouette, a colour palette and a friendly face. None copies an existing franchise character.
- [ ] Each has a gentle idle animation (bobbing, breathing or a sparkle shimmer) and blinks at random intervals.
- [ ] Each has a unique tap dance of about 1–2 seconds that suits who it is (for example, Ember puffs a tiny harmless spark).
- [ ] Each also has a silhouette version for the Collection's not-yet-hatched state.
- [ ] Each Character uses the catalog's art interface, so no screen needs to change when its art is added.
- [ ] They look crisp from small Garden size up to full-screen reveal size, on phone and tablet.
- [ ] The animations stay smooth on a mid-range phone with all 15 Characters in the Garden.
